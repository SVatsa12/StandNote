# Privacy Policy for StandNote Live Transcriber

**Last Updated:** June 2026

## Overview
StandNote Live Transcriber ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our browser extension collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

## Information We Collect
The extension captures audio data from the active browser tab or your microphone solely when you actively initiate a recording session. 

## How We Use Your Information
- **Transcription & Summarization:** The captured audio is temporarily sent to our backend servers strictly for the purpose of converting speech to text and generating meeting summaries.
- **No Permanent Storage of Audio:** We do not permanently store your audio recordings. Once the transcription is processed, the raw audio data is discarded from our processing servers.
- **Local Storage:** The extension uses local browser storage to save your preferences and locally cache transcripts so you do not lose them if the extension popup is closed.

## Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, communications, or audio data to any third parties. Your data is only transmitted to our secure backend to perform the transcription service requested by you.

## Your Consent
By using the StandNote Live Transcriber extension, you consent to our privacy policy and the processing of audio data as described above.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us via our [GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/SVatsa12/StandNote/issues).
